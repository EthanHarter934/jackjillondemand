Our Story

One call. Everything handled.

Jack & Jill On Demand started with a simple idea: people are busy, and life is easier when one trustworthy, local team can handle whatever comes up around the house or business.

Nine years later, we're a family-owned company voted Best of the Best of Bend and a repeat Community Votes winner across 2025 and 2026. That same expansion had us grow beyond Central Oregon to serve all of Oregon and Washington. We offered so much that our clients weren't always sure what "everything" included, so we created specialty brands for catering, events, and personal assisting, all still proudly part of the Jack & Jill family.
